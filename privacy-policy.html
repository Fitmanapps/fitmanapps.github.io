Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

FitMan is a workout app published by Dovdov Dev. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and how you can delete it.

Contact: dovdov.apps@gmail.com

Data stored on your phone

FitMan stores the following on your device: height, weight, date of birth, goals, the physical limitations you select, workouts, food entries, body measurements, water intake, step counts, reminder settings and progress photos.

This data stays on your device unless you choose one of the options below. Photos you add are copied into the app's private storage and are never uploaded.

Cloud backup (optional)

If you sign in with Google and tap Synchronize, your data except progress photos is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore), in an area only your account can reach. Firebase Authentication also stores your Google account name, email address and user ID.

Steps

With your permission (Physical Activity), steps are read on your device through Google Play services or your phone's step sensor. Step data is not sent to us.

Crash reports

When the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics receives a technical report: device model, Android version, app version and the error details. This is on by default so that we can fix problems. You can turn it off in Profile, General Settings.

Usage statistics (optional)

Anonymous usage statistics are sent to Firebase Analytics only if you turn them on in Profile, General Settings. They are off by default.

Ads

The free version of FitMan shows ads from Google AdMob. Some of these ads are supplied by Yandex Mobile Ads, which AdMob calls on our behalf (this is called mediation). Where the law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, you are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Profile, General Settings, Ad privacy choices. Your choice is passed on to Yandex.

To show ads, limit how often you see them and measure them, AdMob and Yandex may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and information about your device and how you interact with the ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Yandex Mobile Ads terms.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Premium removes all ads.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Google Play. We receive the status of your subscription, never your payment details.

Feedback

If you send feedback, your email app sends us your message and your email address. We use them only to reply to you and to improve the app.

What we do not do

We do not sell your data. Apart from the ads described above, we do not share it for advertising.

Service providers

Google LLC provides Firebase, AdMob and Google Play, and may process data on servers outside your country. See the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

YANDEX LLC provides Yandex Mobile Ads, and may process data on servers outside your country. See the Yandex Privacy Policy.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • You can delete your account and cloud backup at any time in the app, or by email if you can no longer open it. See Delete your account.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on your phone.
  • Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days and are then deleted automatically.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, write to dovdov.apps@gmail.com. We process data on the basis of your consent and of our legitimate interest in keeping the app working.

Children

FitMan is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We will publish any changes on this page and update the date at the top.
