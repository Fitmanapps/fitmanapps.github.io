FitMan: lose weight in 30 days

A 30-day bodyweight workout plan for Android. No equipment, a few minutes a day, adjusted to your level.

What the app does

  • A 30-day plan in four stages, at beginner, intermediate or advanced level, that adapts when a workout feels too easy or too hard.
  • Voice guidance, timers and rest periods during each workout, plus your own custom workouts.
  • Weight, water, steps and BMI tracking, with workout and water reminders.
  • Premium adds a food tracker, body measurements and progress photos.
  • Available in English, Russian, Uzbek, German and French.
