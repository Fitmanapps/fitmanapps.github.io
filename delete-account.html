Delete your FitMan account

FitMan (30 Day Weight Loss for Men on Google Play) by Dovdov Dev. You only have an account if you signed in with Google to back up your data.

In the app

  1. Open FitMan and go to Profile, then Backup.
  2. Tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your Google account. The account and its cloud backup are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email dovdov.apps@gmail.com from the Google account you used in FitMan, with the subject "FitMan account deletion". We delete the account and its cloud backup within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your FitMan account: the Google name, email address and user ID linked to it.
  • Your cloud backup: workouts, weight, food entries, body measurements, custom workouts and profile settings.

Nothing from the account is kept afterwards. Crash reports, which contain no personal details, are deleted automatically after 90 days.

Data on your phone

Data stored only on your phone, including progress photos, stays there until you uninstall the app. Uninstalling FitMan removes all of it.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in Google Play, under Payments and subscriptions.
